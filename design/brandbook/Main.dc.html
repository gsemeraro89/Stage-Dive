STAGEDIVE /// BRAND BOOK /// 01 THE BRAND
The insight

Nobody found their favourite band on a clean page. They found it on a photocopied flyer taped to a wall, already third-hand, already a bit ruined.

The idea, in one line
A fanzinethat plays.

Every page is cut, glued and copied by hand. Every picture on it is a full concert, one click away.

Manifesto

No login. No algorithm. No “recommended for you”.
We’ve got scissors, glue and every full gig we could find.
Free, like the flyer on the venue wall.
One click. Jump in.

the crowd will catch you →
The name

Stagedive. The one move in rock where you jump and strangers hold you up. Same deal here: you click, the gig catches you. One word, said the same in every language a festival speaks.

Don’t /// four ways to kill it
STAGEDIVE
Don’t type it. Straight, even letters are a different brand.
STAGEDIVE
Don’t use pink as ink. It is a block behind black, always.
STAGEDIVE
Don’t set words on a photo. Glue a strip down first.
STAGEDIVE
Don’t round corners or soften shadows. Scissors cut straight.
Clear spaceKeep one letter-width of bare paper round the wordmark. Tape may cross that line; nothing else does. Minimum width 160 px full, 90 px flat.
