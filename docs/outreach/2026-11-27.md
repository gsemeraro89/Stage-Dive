## Reddit answers (post from your own account)

1. "Best Wacken sets you can watch in full?" (search: https://www.reddit.com/r/Metal/search/?q=wacken%20full%20show)
   > Uriah Heep at Wacken 2019 (71 minutes) is a joy, and Clutch in 2016 (69 minutes) is the best festival hour they've filmed. For something heavier, Candlemass in 2013 and Testament in 2012 are full shows too. Wacken's own channel has more than 150 of them; I keep them sorted by year with setlists here: https://fullconcert.live/venues/wacken-open-air

2. "Pol'and'Rock / Woodstock Poland concerts worth watching?" (search: https://www.reddit.com/r/Metal/search/?q=woodstock%20poland%20full%20concert)
   > The festival films almost every main-stage set. Arch Enemy in 2018 (71 minutes), Testament in 2019, Prophets of Rage in 2019 (85 minutes) and Skunk Anansie in 2019 are all complete, and Type O Negative's 2007 set (81 minutes) is one of the few full pro-shot shows of theirs. List by year: https://fullconcert.live/venues/polandrock-festival (the pre-2018 editions, under the old name: https://fullconcert.live/venues/przystanek-woodstock)

3. "Any full Type O Negative concert out there?" (search: https://www.reddit.com/r/typeonegative/search/?q=full%20concert)
   > Woodstock Poland 2007: 81 minutes, filmed by the festival itself, pro-shot and complete. It's on the festival's channel; here with the setlist so you can jump to a song: https://fullconcert.live/concerts/type-o-negative-polandrock-festival-przystanek-woodstock-2007

4. "Best stoner/doom festival sets?" (search: https://www.reddit.com/r/stonerrock/search/?q=full%20set%20festival)
   > Hellfest's Valley stage is the place: Fu Manchu 2019 (61 minutes), Clutch 2019, Orange Goblin 2012 are all full sets from the festival's own channel. Freak Valley and Rock Hard festivals have dozens more on WDR Rockpalast. All of them by genre: https://fullconcert.live/genres/stoner

5. "Deafheaven / post-metal live?" (search: https://www.reddit.com/r/Deafheaven/search/?q=live%20full%20set)
   > Their Pitchfork Music Festival set from 2014 (43 minutes, the Sunbather tour) is the full thing, filmed by Pitchfork. Mogwai's Pitchfork Paris 2014 set (67 minutes) is a good companion: https://fullconcert.live/concerts/deafheaven-pitchfork-music-festival-2014
