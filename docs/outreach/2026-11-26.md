## Reddit answers (post from your own account)

1. "Best Metallica concert video?" — search: https://www.reddit.com/r/Metallica/search/?q=best%20full%20concert
   > Slane Castle 2019 is the one everyone ends up at: 2h23, 18 songs, and the crowd in the castle grounds is unreal. If you want the young band, Chicago's Metro 1983 (Kill 'Em All tour, 44 minutes) and Muskegon 1991 (Wherever We May Roam, 2h22) are both full shows. I keep all 55 of their full concerts with setlists in one place: https://fullconcert.live/artists/metallica

2. "Best Radiohead live performance / full show?" — search: https://www.reddit.com/r/radiohead/search/?q=best%20live%20full%20show
   > In Rainbows From the Basement (2008) is the obvious first pick, an hour in a studio and they sound perfect. For a real crowd show, Berlin 2016 at Lollapalooza is two hours and covers A Moon Shaped Pool, and Belfort 1997 catches the OK Computer tour at its peak. Every full show with its setlist: https://fullconcert.live/artists/radiohead

3. "Best pro-shot rock concerts from the 80s/90s?" — search: https://www.reddit.com/r/classicrock/search/?q=full%20concert%20pro%20shot
   > Van Halen at the Montreal Forum 1984 (1h43, Roth at full power), Scorpions in Bremerhaven 1996 and at Seibu Stadium in Japan 1984, and The Police at Hamburg's Markthalle 1980 are all full pro-shot shows worth an evening. Ranked list of the most watched 80s concerts: https://fullconcert.live/best/most-watched-rock-concerts-1980s

4. "Where can I watch full concerts online?" — search: https://www.reddit.com/r/LiveMusic/search/?q=watch%20full%20concerts%20online
   > A lot of festivals and broadcasters put whole shows online officially: WDR Rockpalast, Pinkpop's own channel (sets back to the 90s), Rock Hard and Freak Valley, plus bands like Metallica and Radiohead. I've been collecting them by band, festival and decade with the setlists, so you can click a song and jump to it: https://fullconcert.live

5. "Best festival sets ever filmed?" — search: https://www.reddit.com/r/Music/search/?q=best%20festival%20set%20full
   > Metallica at Slane Castle 2019 and Radiohead in Berlin 2016 for the big ones; Pinkpop's channel has full sets from Radiohead (1996, 2001) to Metallica and Arctic Monkeys. Ranking of the most watched full festival shows: https://fullconcert.live/best/best-festival-rock-concerts
