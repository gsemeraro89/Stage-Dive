## Reddit answers (post from your own account)

1. "Best Bloodstock sets to watch?" (search: https://www.reddit.com/r/Metal/search/?q=bloodstock%20full%20set)
   > The festival films its main stage properly: Megadeth 2017 (86 minutes), Nightwish 2018 (86 minutes), Sabaton 2019 (88 minutes) and Meshuggah 2023 (82 minutes) are complete headline sets. For something nastier, Cannibal Corpse 2018 and Obituary 2017. Over 150 of them, by year and with setlists: https://fullconcert.live/venues/bloodstock-open-air

2. "Slipknot Download 2019 full show?" (search: https://www.reddit.com/r/Slipknot/search/?q=download%202019%20full)
   > It's the full 97-minute set, filmed for Knotfest's own channel, nearly 18 million views. With the setlist so you can jump to a song: https://fullconcert.live/concerts/slipknot-download-festival-2019

3. "Best Gojira live performance?" (search: https://www.reddit.com/r/Gojira/search/?q=best%20live%20full%20set)
   > Bloodstock 2016 is a great one, an hour on the main stage during the Magma era, filmed by the festival. Setlist and the other full shows: https://fullconcert.live/concerts/gojira-bloodstock-open-air-2016

4. "Any good full Megadeth concert from the Dystopia era?" (search: https://www.reddit.com/r/Megadeth/search/?q=full%20concert%202017)
   > Bloodstock 2017: 86 minutes, Dystopia tour, pro-shot by the festival. Setlist here: https://fullconcert.live/concerts/megadeth-bloodstock-open-air-2017

5. "The Hives live, best full show?" (search: https://www.reddit.com/r/TheHives/search/?q=full%20concert)
   > The band's own channel has Les Vieilles Charrues 2008 (66 minutes) and Lollapalooza Chile 2013 (68 minutes), both complete. All their full shows with setlists: https://fullconcert.live/artists/the-hives
